Euclidean Algorithm (Calculationg the GCD)

Input data:

$$ \begin{array}{cc} a = 20405 & \quad \quad b = 84645 \\ \end{array} $$

Calculation:

$$ \begin{array}{cc} x = \left|a\right| & \quad \quad y = \left|b\right| \\ \end{array} $$
$$\begin{array}{l} \textbf{while } x \ne 0 \;\land\; y \ne 0 \textbf{ do} \\ \left| \begin{array}{l} \quad \textbf{if } x > y \textbf{ then} \\ \quad \left| \begin{array}{l} \quad x = x \bmod y \end{array} \right.\\ \quad \textbf{else} \\ \quad \left| \begin{array}{l} \quad y = y \bmod x \end{array} \right. \end{array} \right. \end{array}$$
$$GCD = x + y$$

Result:

$$GCD = 55$$

Control:

$$ \begin{array}{cc} \frac{a}{GCD} = 371 & \quad \quad \frac{b}{GCD} = 1539 \\ \end{array} $$
