$$L = 20\text{ ft} = 20 \text{ ft}$$
$$w = 0.5 \frac{\text{kip}}{\text{in}} = 0.5 \dfrac{\text{kip}}{\text{in}}$$
$$E = 29000\text{ ksi} = 29000 \text{ ksi}$$
$$I = 758\text{ in}^{4} = 758 \text{ in}^{4}$$
$$delta = \frac{5 \cdot w \cdot L^{4}}{384 \cdot E \cdot I} = 0.082 \text{ ft}$$
