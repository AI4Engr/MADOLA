Numeric integration method (Simpson's rule)

Input data:

$$\begin{array}{l} \textbf{function } f(x) \\ \left| \begin{array}{l} \quad \text{return } x^{2} \end{array} \right. \end{array}$$
$$a = 1$$
$$b = 5$$
$$n = 1$$

Calculation:

$$h = \frac{b - a}{n}$$
$$int = \frac{1}{3} \cdot \left(\frac{h}{2} \cdot \left(f(a) + f(b)\right) + h \cdot \sum_{k=1}^{n - 1} f(a + k \cdot h)\right) + \frac{2}{3} \cdot h \cdot \sum_{k=1}^{n} f(a - \frac{h}{2} + k \cdot h)$$

Result:

$$int = 41.333$$

Control:

$$control = \frac{b^{3}}{3} - \frac{a^{3}}{3}$$
$$control = 41.333$$
